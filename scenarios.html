Scenarios

Real moments from a collector's life, and how Too Many Comics changes them. These aren't hypotheticals. Every one comes from using the app with a real collection. Each scenario is its own page: pick a moment you know.

The convention table Your table becomes a mobile comic book store A searchable, offline kiosk of your show stock, and no price stickers ever again. The stack and the offer “I'll give you eighty bucks for these.” Scan the stack, see the asking total, record the sale, all before the line grows. The other side of the table “Would you want to buy these?” Photograph their books, see what you own and what the market says, then offer. No deal in sight What are these, and do I have them? Identify mystery books and settle “do I have this?” right at the bin. The phone call “Do you buy collections? I'm about an hour away.” A collection an hour away, scanned by the seller and reviewed from your desk. Dealer to dealer Selling to someone who speaks the same language AirDrop the books, records and labels included. Both inventories stay right. Among friends The book I didn't need, but Todd did Friends' and clients' collections in your pocket: want lists, consignments, answers. After the show Getting the boxes back to the truth Audit Box reconciles what sold, what moved, and what walked. The listing desk Selling a few. To make room for more, of course. An auction pack that drafts your eBay listings and syncs them back. The back-issue bin Wait. Do I already own this? Point the camera at a cover and know if you own it, printing and all. The grading decision Should I get this comic professionally graded? Eight photos and one honest AI verdict, before you spend $30 and six weeks. The morning check Buzz is what moves a book overnight A morning sweep of comic news, matched to the books in your boxes.
