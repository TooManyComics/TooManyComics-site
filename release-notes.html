Release notes

Every build that reaches a tester gets an entry here. What changed is what it says, no gloss.

0.1.0 · First TestFlight beta

The first build to leave the developer's own phone.

Everything is new in this one, so this entry is an inventory of what ships rather than a list of changes:

  • Scan and identify. Point the camera at a cover. The app identifies the book, suggests a grade from your photos, and estimates a value. Corrections are free.
  • Advanced Grading. Front, back, spine, and corners become a defect-by-defect condition report with a suggested grade, best appreciated on a book in rough shape.
  • Ballpark values. Price a whole stack in batches, and refresh estimates later when the market moves.
  • Hot Books. A daily look at books moving in the market, with sources credited and linked.
  • Credits. A free weekly allowance for everyone, credit packs for heavy weeks. During the beta, purchases run in Apple's sandbox and charge nothing.
  • Private by design. No account, no login. Your collection lives on your phone; the server sees only the single photo being analyzed.
  • Backup and restore. Export the whole collection to an archive and bring it back, on this phone or the next one.

Known in this build: iPhone only, though it installs on iPad in compatibility mode. Scanning and pricing need a network connection; browsing your collection does not.

Found something broken? Support has the address. A screenshot and the rough time it happened are usually enough for us to find it.
